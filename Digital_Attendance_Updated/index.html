Offline and free-plan notes

While offline, records remain in this device’s IndexedDB and show as waiting to sync. When internet returns, this page retries automatically. If two devices both record the same employee while offline, the first record accepted by the shared database wins; the other device will replace its pending copy with the shared record on sync. Free hosting limits and pauses can delay syncing, so keep JSON backups.
